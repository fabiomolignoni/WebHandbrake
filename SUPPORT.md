# Support

## Look it up first

- **Help**, in the dashboard, explains every part of WebHandbrake and works offline. The same
  text is in the [user guide](docs/user-guide.md); start with
  [Troubleshooting](docs/user-guide.md#troubleshooting).
- **Why?** in the popup, and **Test a URL** in the rule editor, show which condition applies to a
  page and when that changes.

## Locked out

If your rules or protection level turned out to be too strict, the emergency exit always works and
needs nobody else: see [Emergency exit](docs/user-guide.md#emergency-exit).

## Ask a question or report a problem

- **Questions**:
  [GitHub Discussions, category Q&A](https://github.com/fabiomolignoni/WebHandbrake/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Ideas and feature requests**:
  [GitHub Discussions, category Ideas](https://github.com/fabiomolignoni/WebHandbrake/discussions/categories/ideas).
  An idea that the maintainer accepts becomes an issue.
- **Something does not work**: the
  [bug report form](https://github.com/fabiomolignoni/WebHandbrake/issues/new/choose).
- **An accessibility barrier**: the **Accessibility problem** form on the same page.

WebHandbrake is a volunteer project: replies are given on a best-effort basis, with no promised
response time.

## What to include

- The WebHandbrake version, shown in **Help › About**.
- The browser and its version, and the operating system.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- If it helps, the diagnostic report from **Settings › Diagnostics › Copy diagnostic report**.
  Addresses are removed from it unless you tick **Include addresses (off by default)**.

## Never post

- Your browsing history or the addresses you visit, unless they are needed and you are happy to
  make them public.
- Export files, which contain your rules and settings, and possibly your statistics and the hash
  of your settings password.
- Passwords.

## Security and conduct

- A security problem is never reported in public: follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- To report behaviour that breaks the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), use **Report
  content › Report to repository admins** on the issue, pull request, discussion or comment.
